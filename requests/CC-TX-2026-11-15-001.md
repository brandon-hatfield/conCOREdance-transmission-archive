Body:
Retrospective record published November 17, 2026, covering the November 14–15 development work. These features were uncommitted at that checkpoint and are now preserved within complete local source commit 9cbbb6aaec864915441f43027442ed270575dc43. That commit is a later accumulated checkpoint, not an original November 15 commit, and this archive update does not publish the private app source.

Encrypted reusable progress-note templates support revisions, defaults and session/audio-draft use. Notes preserve their applied template snapshot. Document Preview recognizes section headings. Optional clinician signature images are applied deliberately at finalization and retained with the final note; amendments start unsigned. New-patient onboarding can be saved and resumed.

The dashboard uses one randomized reflection area, current-day scheduling and fewer promotional/demo panels. New sessions no longer receive canned continuity entries; existing entries remain intact.

Encryption envelope v2 introduced PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, authenticated metadata and decrypt-only legacy compatibility. Historical vaults open at an explicit read-only gate. Updating verifies a complete encrypted recovery backup and original documents before committing the updated vault. Password-only recovery remains a stated limitation; biometrics do not replace the backup password. This is an implemented format and migration path, not an independent cryptographic review.

The historical full suite passed 213 tests with two optional integrations skipped and zero failures. A separately enabled twenty-test Apple speech/model suite passed with no skips, using synthetic inputs. Selected synthetic native screens were inspected. These are November 15 development results, not tests rerun for this archive publication.

The then-current isolated development preview was later archived during the November 16 launch cleanup. It is not the current acceptance app. November 17 storage schema 3 is a later change, distinct from encryption envelope v2.

Independent security/recovery review, physical microphone/call capture, comprehensive biometric failure handling, accessibility and clinical workflow acceptance, and durable signed distribution remained open. No patient records, credentials, private signing material or personal imagery accompany this public record.
