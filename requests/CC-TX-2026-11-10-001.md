Summary:
Apple audio-to-note development workflow is implemented; 178 tests and a real synthetic-text Apple model call pass. Native audio, permissions and screen acceptance remain open.

Body:
The required microphone plus call-audio workflow is implemented as a development feature. The session screen has explicit participant-consent acknowledgement and Start/Stop controls, Apple's native call-app picker, English speech-model setup/readiness and clinician transcript review. ScreenCaptureKit registers audio and microphone outputs only. Microphone and Call audio are source labels, not inferred speaker identities. Raw audio is transient in app memory; the app has no raw-audio file writer.

SpeechAnalyzer/SpeechTranscriber adapters handle separate source streams, PCM conversion, bounded input queues and finalized timed segments. Transcripts are limited to two hours, 200,000 characters and 10,000 segments; interruption/overflow is surfaced. Finalized text is checkpointed inside the encrypted vault. Reopening or restoring an abandoned capture makes it interrupted and reviewable, without rewriting the encrypted file merely to unlock it.

Apple Intelligence drafting uses bounded Foundation Models sessions and quoted transcript evidence. Generated source IDs and exact quotes are verified, but that does not establish the clinical correctness of every paraphrase. The owner reviews the transcript before generation, then edits/reviews the candidate before creating an unfinalized note in the selected Narrative, SOAP, DAP, BIRP or GIRP format. Existing session notes are not overwritten. Created notes preserve a source transcript ID, revision, full reviewed-text snapshot and facts; later corrections do not change that note's source history, and amendments retain the provenance.

Privacy protection immediately closes a thread-safe native callback gate and invalidates transcription/generation operations, then waits for tracked native teardown and analysis cleanup. A normal stop and privacy-triggered stop share the same OS-stop operation; restart remains blocked while cleanup is pending. Failed stops are reported and transcripts marked interrupted. Late results cannot modify a protected, re-unlocked or changed vault session. Inactivity protection remains enabled unless explicitly paused for that capture; screen lock, sleep and manual protection still stop capture. This is UI/operation protection, not a claim of cryptographic memory erasure while buffers or Apple services finish processing.

The fresh final full XCTest suite passed 178 tests with zero failures and no skips, including nineteen audio tests. These cover controlled microphone/call sources, consent/review gates, encrypted save/backup recovery, immutable source/amendment provenance, interrupted checkpoints, explicit idle-pause behavior, cancellation, changed transcripts, late results, failed/suspended native stops and grounding/chunking checks. Apple's actual on-device language model was invoked with a simple synthetic transcript and produced source-validated facts and a DAP draft. This real model call is distinct from the mocked audio source/transcription checks; it is not clinical-quality validation.

Development bundle 20261110152745 built and passed strict ad hoc signature verification, identifying implementation commit 1be9b62. Complete source and final verification documentation are preserved in the private repository on feature/apple-session-audio-2026-11-09 at commit 5ed878ba443d1f2beed029d9c21ebfb497e58413. The final documentation checkpoint has the same Sources tree, d0290d06f74bbc1ee6397e7da16839fb969b1061, as the packaged implementation. The source is proposed in a draft PR stacked on the PDF-intake branch and is not merged or released.

Native screen review could not be completed: computer-use app-state access timed out for both the prior synthetic preview and a fresh audio-bundle launch, including after resetting the computer-use session. The bundle process launched with an isolated synthetic fixture, but no visual acceptance is claimed. The cause of those access timeouts is unestablished.

Runtime capability checks found Apple Intelligence available and SpeechTranscriber supported on this Mac, with English speech assets supported but not installed. Real microphone/call capture and actual SpeechTranscriber output have not been verified. Model setup, macOS permission grants, consented dual-source capture, echo/timing and device changes, long sessions, sleep/wake, representative clinical-content/model-refusal checks and native screen acceptance remain a hands-on gate. No real clinical record, actual call, microphone recording or biometric credential was used.

This supersedes the November 9 statement that audio-to-note development was pending implementation. It does not close the new clinical-beta acceptance gate. Reviewed key derivation/recovery, independent security/failure review, trusted distribution, operations and broader clinical acceptance remain open. The app remains a synthetic-data development preview and is not approved for real clinical records. AUDIO_NOTE_VERIFICATION.md and BETA_READINESS.md contain the exact implementation and remaining checks.
