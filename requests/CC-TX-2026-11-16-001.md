Retrospective record published November 17, 2026, from the November 16 implementation and owner-verification notes. Client details can be corrected without replacing the stable client identity or rewriting authored clinical snapshots. Duplicate merging requires a surviving record, explicit field choices, a reason and typed confirmation. Clinical documents, notes, assessments, sessions and related history are preserved, with the original profile retained in encrypted merged history. Deletion requires explicit confirmation and a reason; failed writes roll back, and referenced originals are cleaned up only after a successful save. Existing backups and external exports are retained.

The client directory opens a single chart. Actions wrap, metadata columns adapt, and secondary content moves below primary work when space is constrained. Native synthetic portrait, landscape and larger-text renders were inspected. The local record reports a 237-test full run with five optional skips and zero failures, followed by a 32-test focused pass without failures or skips. Those are historical November 16 records; the November 17 counts are reported separately.

Release-authorization tracking records actual request, receipt, clinician review, disclosure, revocation and expiration events and preserves referenced originals. Google eSignature preparation/sending remains an explicit external handoff, not automatic collection or response ingestion. Filed assessment PDFs are visible without inventing structured assessment answers. Locally generated lock/unlock feedback is optional. These workflow changes are development implementations, not legal-validity or compliance certification.

An owner-authorized installed checkpoint completed the explicit v2 clinical-vault update, reopened existing records, persisted a demographic correction and completed an attachment-inclusive encrypted backup. An older installed app could not read the new encryption envelope and was still being launched through an outdated route. The current user-level app was reopened, the Dock route corrected, and obsolete application bundles were subsequently archived at the owner's request. No vault restoration was used to resolve that launch incident.

This corrects the older archive's blanket current-state description of the entire project as synthetic-only: the owner used an installed clinical checkpoint. It does not establish general clinical-release approval. The later November 17 acceptance package uses separate synthetic storage and does not update that owner vault to schema 3. No clinical identifiers, vault contents, private paths or credentials are included here.

- Treat receipt, review and disclosure as distinct clinician-confirmed events.
- Retire obsolete launch routes without deleting vaults or recovery backups.
- Distinguish owner use from independent acceptance and distribution approval.
