Body:
Actual Apple SpeechAnalyzer/SpeechTranscriber was exercised with a short locally generated synthetic English speech file, paced through the microphone and call source adapters. No microphone or real call was recorded. Apple asset setup was attempted through the public API; the first run returned CancellationError. Subsequent streaming exposed the underlying audio timestamp failure: independently rounded capture timestamps could overlap preceding resampled buffers, which SpeechAnalyzer rejects.

Each source track now schedules input no earlier than the exact end of its previous converted buffer while preserving genuine gaps. The real regression test failed before the correction and passed afterward, producing recognizable breathing-practice text from both source tracks. This verifies actual two-stream speech processing and PCM conversion; source labels do not establish speaker identity. English asset readiness must still be checked in the installed app context.

The final full suite passed 179 tests with zero failures and no skips, including twenty audio tests. Both real Apple integration checks were explicitly enabled: generated-audio transcription and synthetic-text Foundation Models note drafting. The earlier 178-test milestone remains a historical checkpoint; this entry supersedes its unverified SpeechTranscriber-output status.

Physical microphone/call capture, ScreenCaptureKit permissions, native screen acceptance, echo/timing, long sessions, device changes, sleep and permission revocation remain open. Prior computer-use timeouts still prevent visual acceptance; no cause is established. Clinical-quality and independent security/recovery/signing gates remain open. This is an unmerged synthetic-data development preview, not approval for clinical records.

Decisions:
- Preserve separate monotonic audio timelines after conversion.
- Distinguish real synthetic-audio ASR from physical capture acceptance.

Next Actions:
