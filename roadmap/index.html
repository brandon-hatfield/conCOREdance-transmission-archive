01

The record workflow

Implemented development capabilities, with automated and selected native synthetic verification. Implementation does not close the acceptance gates below.

Implemented

Encrypted local records

Local vaults, password and biometric-unlock implementation, explicit locking, encrypted originals and audit events. Retained editors behind a privacy cover are distinct from a fully cleared vault.

Client and caseload management

Onboarding, demographic corrections, explicit duplicate merges/deletion, selectable columns, saved sorting and search. Original profiles and clinical snapshots are preserved where required.

Reviewed documentation

Reusable templates, readable note sections, deliberate signature images, final notes and linked amendments. Embedded BPS assessment drafts, review, finalization and preserved payloads.

Documents and authorizations

Bounded PDF intake, original preservation and client filing. Release tracking separates sending, receipt, clinician review and disclosure; Google eSignature remains an explicit external handoff.

Backup and portable output

Attachment-inclusive encrypted backups export locally to Downloads. Client packets contain readable notes, structured JSON and originals. Same-Mac synthetic recovery passed; automatic Google Drive upload is not implemented.

Therapist-controlled partner choice

Choose a known or custom billing/admin partner, change it later or clear the association. Bundled thumbnails work offline; choosing a partner does not connect to its platform.

The November 17 candidate uses record schema 3 under envelope-v2 encryption. Schema-1/2 vaults require an explicit verified-backup update. The owner’s existing clinical installation was not changed by this acceptance pass.

02

Close the beta acceptance gates

The immediate priority. The separate acceptance app uses synthetic records; broader beta distribution and clinical-release approval remain open.

Current priority

Native authentication and updates

Owner-observed Touch ID enrollment, cancellation, password fallback, relaunch and update behavior. Verify the intended vault, source revision and complete install/update path.

Consented live audio

Check physical microphone and telehealth-call capture, permissions, devices, timing/echo, long sessions and sleep/lock interruption. Synthetic Apple integrations passed; intermittent model behavior still needs reliability follow-up.

A complete clinical walkthrough

Onboarding → assessment → session → reviewed note → deliberate signature → amendment → export. Check long notes, laptop/portrait/desktop layouts, keyboard access and VoiceOver.

Independent recovery and security

Restore on a clean independent destination/device and review cryptography, key management, failure behavior and recovery instructions. Test filesystem, scale and power-loss limits.

Source review and preservation

Complete local source is checkpointed. Independent code review and remote preservation of the latest private app commits remain separate work; publishing this website does not publish that source.

Durable distribution trust

The candidate is Apple Development-signed, not notarized. Its profile expires November 20, 2026 at 9:01 p.m. Eastern. Establish durable signing/notarization, supported updates and operating guidance.

03

Coordinate without confinement

Planned work, with scope and architecture to be settled. These are not shipped integrations or release-date commitments.

Future direction

Partner-specific documentation profiles

Partners may have different formats, fields and submission requirements. Future profiles need verified sources, effective dates and revisions, with therapist inspection and deliberate application.

Historical service attribution

Preserve the partner and profile used for a service/document rather than retroactively relabeling prior work when the current association changes. Per-service attribution automation is not implemented.

Practice administration and provider identities

Separate clinician authentication, record permissions, audit attribution and encryption/storage boundaries. Shared-terminal handoff must protect unsaved work and clear the prior provider’s visible context.

Broader imports and integrations

Vendor migration, additional provider layers, welcome packets and eSignature response ingestion need explicit workflows and appropriate privacy boundaries. Core record access remains independent of them.

Configurable backup destinations

Google Drive backup remains planned. A future destination workflow needs explicit configuration, clear copy/upload or sync confirmation, failure reporting and verified restore. The current app writes local encrypted backups.

Further clinical structure

Continue evaluating treatment-plan, safety/risk and continuity workflows against actual clinician needs. Draft assistance must preserve source grounding and clinician authorship.

Release and support readiness

Independent review, accepted workflows, recovery/user guides, privacy documentation, a support process and a final feedback cycle precede general or commercial availability.

Changing a partner must not rewrite signed documentation, manufacture findings or imply that a checklist guarantees acceptance by a platform. Shared practice administration is distinct from clinical-supervision access.
