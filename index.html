02 /Record ownership

The memory should
stay with you.

The product’s foundations are practical: keep the record accessible, preserve what was authored, and make leaving possible.

Explore the design doctrine ↗
01

Local first.

The core record lives in an encrypted vault on the Mac. Access to clinical memory should not depend on a continuing subscription.

02

Clinician-authored.

Templates and draft assistance support documentation. Deliberate review, signature snapshots and linked amendments preserve clinical authorship.

03

Portable by design.

Complete backups include original documents. Readable packets and structured exports make record ownership more than a promise.

04

Your choice of partners.

Therapist and client choose the billing/admin association. Change it later, use a custom partner, or leave it unassigned.
