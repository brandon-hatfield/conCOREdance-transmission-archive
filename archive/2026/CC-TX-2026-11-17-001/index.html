Sortable Caseload, Partner Choice and Standalone Recovery Acceptance

This November 17 development checkpoint preserves complete local app source in 9cbbb6aaec864915441f43027442ed270575dc43 and subsequent partner-choice/roadmap documentation in d8e98860c5b5ea9cde2f932d87ed8f95dac5d2f6. Both are local commits on codex/readability-and-practice-branding. This public archive update does not push, merge or distribute the private application source.

The caseload has a fixed Client column and optional contact, birth-date, upcoming/completed-session, note-count, onboarding and client-since columns. Headers and a picker sort in either direction; dates and counts use their actual values, with unknown dates last. Column visibility and sorting persist locally. Search includes client/contact/partner text; narrow layouts scroll horizontally and preserve chosen text size.

Billing/admin partner is editable after onboarding: therapist and client may choose a preset, enter a custom partner, change it later or clear it. Presets do not restrict that choice. Small bundled official icons identify SonderMind, Headway, Rula, BetterHelp, Alma, Grow Therapy, Talkspace, Spring Health, Lyra Health and Modern Health; names remain available on hover and to accessibility. Direct/private practice and custom partners use neutral icons. No runtime logo lookup or partner integration is implied. Assignments are preserved through edits, merge choices, encrypted recovery and portable exports.

Record storage schema 3 prevents older schema-2 readers from silently discarding partner metadata. Encryption remains envelope v2. Opening a schema-1/2 vault requires the explicit backup-verified format-update gate. The owner-installed clinical checkpoint was not replaced or migrated during this pass.

A combined synthetic scenario created onboarding, a custom template, a deliberately signed final note, a linked amendment, assessment JSON and a filed original PDF. The source directory was moved away before restoring the standalone encrypted backup into a clean destination. Reopen checks and independently extracted portable packets verified record associations, readable notes and byte-identical signature, assessment and original files. This is same-Mac automated recovery, not an independent-device or actual clinical-backup restore.

Earlier in the same development pass, an expanded schema-2 suite passed 244 tests without failures or skips, including all optional renders and actual Apple speech/model integrations. After adding the schema-3 guard, the full suite passed 245 tests with four optional skips and zero failures; the skipped checks had passed in that preceding run. Final focused caseload checks passed six tests without skips or failures. These tests were run during app development, not rerun during archive publication. Native synthetic narrow, portrait, landscape and 175-percent text renders were inspected.

One Apple model request failed despite an available status, then passed in an isolated repeat and the expanded run. That intermittent behavior is recorded rather than treated as resolved reliability. Existing notes/transcripts are retained after failed generation.

A separate Apple Development-signed acceptance bundle, build 20261117200902 from 9cbbb6a, passed deep/strict signature verification, storage-pin checks and all ten bundled-icon fingerprints. It was not launched or installed over the clinical application. Its development profile expires November 20, 2026 at 9:01:01 p.m. Eastern. No notarized or Developer ID distribution is asserted.

Future partner-specific documentation profiles are earmarked, not implemented. Requirements may vary by partner and over time; future profiles need verified sources, effective dates and revisions, deliberate therapist application and preservation of historical service attribution and signed content. Changing a current partner does not rewrite final notes, and per-service partner-history automation is not implemented. Shared practice/provider identities remain separate future architecture work.

The public verification inventory records scope and fingerprints without clinical records, passwords, private file locations or signing material. Remaining beta gates are native launch/update, Touch ID and consented physical audio checks; permission/device/long-session/sleep behavior; clinical/accessibility review; independent device recovery and security review; source review/remote preservation and durable signed distribution.

Decisions

  • Keep partner association editable and open to custom choices.
  • Use offline thumbnails with accessible names rather than text-heavy partner cells.
  • Separate storage schema 3 from envelope-v2 encryption and enforce explicit updates.
  • Preserve prior signed documentation when current partner associations change.
  • Track partner-specific documentation profiles as future work, not present integration.

Next Actions

  • Run the isolated candidate's native owner walkthrough, including Touch ID, full documentation/export and consented audio capture.
  • Verify independent-device recovery and commission independent security review.
  • Resolve development signing before profile expiration; validate install/update and notarized distribution.
  • Review and remotely preserve the complete private source checkpoint without exposing clinical material.
